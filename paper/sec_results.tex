\paragraph{What 30\% of the parameters costs.}
Partition decides the level. The dense network scores \num{resisc45:dense_ce:novel_5}\% on RESISC45 at 5 shots, \num{eurosat13:dense_ce:novel_5}\% on EuroSAT-MS and \num{plantvillage:dense_ce:novel_5}\% on PlantVillage, with single partitions ranging from \num{resisc45:dense_ce:novel_5:min}\% to \num{resisc45:dense_ce:novel_5:max}\% on RESISC45 and from \num{eurosat13:dense_ce:novel_5:min}\% to \num{eurosat13:dense_ce:novel_5:max}\% on EuroSAT-MS (Table~\ref{tab:scores}). Keeping $30\%$ of the parameters with DACIS under PMP costs \num{d:rs:novel_5:dense-dacisPMP:abs} points on RESISC45 \ci{rs:novel_5:dense-dacisPMP}, \num{d:es:novel_5:dense-dacisPMP:abs} on EuroSAT-MS \ci{es:novel_5:dense-dacisPMP} and \num{d:pv:novel_5:dense-dacisPMP:abs} on PlantVillage \ci{pv:novel_5:dense-dacisPMP}; the brackets are $95\%$ intervals over partitions. On PlantDoc field photographs the same PlantVillage networks fall from \num{plantvillage:dense_ce:plantdoc_5}\% to \num{plantvillage:A_dacis_PMP_30:plantdoc_5}\%.

\paragraph{The choice among scores is a small effect.}
Under PMP the best and the worst of the eight scores are \num{spread:rs:novel_5:PMP} points apart on RESISC45, \num{spread:es:novel_5:PMP} on EuroSAT-MS, \num{spread:pv:novel_5:PMP} on PlantVillage and \num{spread:pd:plantdoc_5:PMP} on PlantDoc, where the standard deviation over partitions of the partition-mean accuracy is \num{partsd:rs:novel_5:PMP}, \num{partsd:es:novel_5:PMP}, \num{partsd:pv:novel_5:PMP} and \num{partsd:pd:plantdoc_5:PMP}. Random selection ranks \num{randrank:rs:novel_5:PMP}, \num{randrank:es:novel_5:PMP}, \num{randrank:pv:novel_5:PMP} and \num{randrank:pd:plantdoc_5:PMP} of eight on the four settings (1 is best). Of the \num{cmp:total:PMP} paired comparisons of a computed score with random selection (seven scores, four settings), \num{cmp:above:PMP} have a $95\%$ interval above zero and \num{cmp:below:PMP} below it (\num{cmp:belowlist:PMP}); with that many intervals about \num{cmp:chance:PMP} would exclude zero by chance alone, and the largest mean advantage of a computed score is \num{cmp:maxgain:PMP} points. Under schedule P the counts are \num{cmp:above:P} and \num{cmp:below:P}. DACIS minus random is \num{d:rs:novel_5:dacis-random:PMP} points on RESISC45 \ci{rs:novel_5:dacis-random:PMP} and \num{d:es:novel_5:dacis-random:PMP} on EuroSAT-MS \ci{es:novel_5:dacis-random:PMP}; DACIS beats random in \num{d:rs:novel_5:dacis-random:PMP:wins} of \num{d:rs:novel_5:dacis-random:PMP:n} RESISC45 partitions and in \num{d:es:novel_5:dacis-random:PMP:wins} of \num{d:es:novel_5:dacis-random:PMP:n} EuroSAT-MS partitions (Table~\ref{tab:pairs}). Figure~\ref{fig:seeds} shows why the intervals are wide: a score that leads on one partition trails on another, and Kendall's coefficient of concordance for the ranking across partitions is only \num{kw:rs:novel_5:PMP} on RESISC45 and \num{kw:es:novel_5:PMP} on EuroSAT-MS, where unrelated rankings would give about $1/\num{resisc45:seeds}$. The channels the scores keep are far from identical (Jaccard overlap in the supplement), so the null result does not come from the scores choosing the same channels. At 1 and 10 shots no computed score is clearly above random selection either (supplement).

\paragraph{Episodic training did not help.}
On the dense network, adding $1000$ episodes ($800$ on EuroSAT-MS) of prototypical training after cross-entropy pre-training changes 5-shot accuracy by \num{d:rs:novel_5:densem-dense} points on RESISC45 \ci{rs:novel_5:densem-dense}, \num{d:es:novel_5:densem-dense} on EuroSAT-MS \ci{es:novel_5:densem-dense} and \num{d:pv:novel_5:densem-dense} on PlantVillage \ci{pv:novel_5:densem-dense}, at the learning rate chosen on validation classes (\num{tune:best}). The validation sweep in the supplement falls as the learning rate grows on both sets that have validation classes, and no value beats stopping after S0. Inside pruning schedules the stage does not pay for itself either (Table~\ref{tab:pairs}): P$\to$M differs from P by \num{d:rs:novel_5:PM-P} points on RESISC45, PMP by \num{d:rs:novel_5:PMP-P}, and the gradient probe P$\to$G$\to$P, which collects $G_{\mathrm{meta}}$ without updating a weight, by \num{d:rs:novel_5:PGP-P}. Giving M twice the episodes changes nothing that the PlantVillage partitions can resolve. Guo et al.~\cite{guo2020bscd} report the same sign for meta-learning methods on crop-disease and EuroSAT targets.

\paragraph{Budget and cost.}
Figure~\ref{fig:budget} shows where the curves bend, and Table~\ref{tab:budget} puts the cost next to the accuracy and next to three off-the-shelf small networks trained with the same recipe. A ResNet-18 pruned to $30\%$ of its parameters needs \num{resisc45:A_l1_PMP_30:macs_128} G multiply-accumulates at $128{\times}128$ against \num{resisc45:dense_ce:macs_128} G for the dense network, which is \num{cost:macs-red:30}\% fewer, but its single-thread CPU latency is only \num{cost:cpu-red:30}\% lower. We did not profile layer by layer. The stem, the shortcuts and the residual widths are untouched, and at batch size one the small layers may be limited by memory traffic and call overhead more than by arithmetic; either would explain the gap, and we cannot tell which. MobileNetV3-Small has \num{cost:mobilenet_v3_small:params} M parameters and needs \num{cost:mobilenet_v3_small:macs} G multiply-accumulates; it scores \num{resisc45:K_mobilenet_v3_small:novel_5}\% on RESISC45 and \num{eurosat13:K_mobilenet_v3_small:novel_5}\% on EuroSAT-MS against \num{resisc45:A_dacis_PMP_30:novel_5}\% and \num{eurosat13:A_dacis_PMP_30:novel_5}\% for the pruned ResNet-18.

\paragraph{Sentinel-2 bands.}
On EuroSAT-MS the dense network with all thirteen bands scores \num{eurosat13:dense_ce:novel_5}\% at 5 shots against \num{eurosat3:dense_ce:novel_5}\% for the same network on the three visible bands (Table~\ref{tab:eurosat}). With five base classes and ImageNet filters for the visible bands only, the extra bands did not help. The comparison between scores looks the same with thirteen bands and with three.

\paragraph{Recovery and the parts of DACIS (PlantVillage).}
Longer recovery after pruning helps more than any score: with six epochs instead of one, random selection and filter $\ell_1$ reach \num{plantvillage:R_random_P_30_r6:novel_5}\% and \num{plantvillage:R_l1_P_30_r6:novel_5}\% under schedule P on PlantVillage, against \num{plantvillage:P_random:novel_5}\% and \num{plantvillage:P_l1:novel_5}\% with one. Of the \num{abl:count} variants of DACIS in the supplement (single components, pairs, no meta-gradient term, other depth profile or scaling), the largest mean change in PlantVillage PMP accuracy is \num{abl:max} points, \num{abl:maxsign} the full score, for \num{abl:maxname}; \num{abl:nsig} of them have a $95\%$ interval clear of zero. \label{sec:lambda}On the validation classes, the eight weightings of Eq.~\eqref{eq:dacis} that we tried span \num{lam:val5:worst}\% to \num{lam:val5:best}\% at 5 shots against \num{lam:val5:default}\% for the weights fixed in advance, so we left $\lambda$ alone.
